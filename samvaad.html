§ 02 · About

Why Policy Samvaad

I founded Policy Samvaad as an independent public policy publication. Its editorial agenda connects governance, institutional reform and economic development with legal analysis.

The essays look at how international policy models can be adapted to Indian contexts. I use comparative analysis to examine the institutional assumptions behind a policy and the implementation capacity it needs.

It is written for professional audiences. Each piece turns a legal or governance question into a concise essay for public policy discussion.
