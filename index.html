§ 01 · About

A lawyer and public policy adviser for healthcare innovation

I help founders translate regulatory requirements into practical policies, partnership safeguards and business decisions.

I'm Preethi Gowda, a lawyer and public policy adviser based in Bengaluru. I work with healthcare technology startups, including teams developing deeptech applications, on compliance, due diligence, governance and strategy informed by law and public policy.

My experience at LEAR Medical and MediBlock includes healthcare regulatory analysis, internal policies, institutional onboarding and digital health governance. I hold an LL.M. in International Business and Economic Law from Georgetown University Law Center and am pursuing a Post Graduate Diploma in Medical Law and Ethics at NLSIU.

My earlier research at Georgetown and the Stimson Center adds a comparative perspective to the legal and policy questions founders face.

§ 03 · Selected work

Three examples of the work

Each one sets out what I did and what it produced.

i.MediBlock · Regulatory intelligence

Healthcare regulatory intelligence

I mapped healthcare laws across all 50 US states and ten policy categories and converted the findings into structured policy intelligence. I worked with legal, engineering, GIS and policy colleagues to make licensing, telehealth and data governance requirements usable in digital workflows.

ShowsRegulatory research, structured synthesis and work with technical teams.

ii.LEAR Medical · Compliance

Compliance and onboarding

I developed internal data protection and client engagement policies, onboarding procedures and contractual safeguards. I reviewed Indian and UK healthcare regulations and prepared due diligence guidance for institutional relationships.

ShowsTranslating legal requirements into documentation, responsibilities and operating processes.

iii.LEAR Medical · Governance

Diagnostic safety governance

I applied comparative analysis of aviation safety principles to radiology governance, connecting peer review, root cause analysis and quality monitoring with institutional accountability. I prepared policy briefs for hospital networks and government engagements.

ShowsHealthcare governance analysis, explained to institutional audiences.

The research behind the practice. At Georgetown I examined digital regulation and international economic governance. At the Stimson Center my work covered trade policy, Global South development and project-finance due diligence. That is where the comparative and institutional perspective in my advisory work comes from.
